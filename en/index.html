Lucio Freni

Writer · Rome, Italy

Independent Italian writer, neurodivergent. Asperger's, ADHD, and the kind of intelligence that lets you see every connection but can't find the door out of the room. Seven published books: noir, dystopian satire, philosophical grotesque, war epistolary fiction, neuroscience essays. I write because if I didn't, it would be as if I had never existed. My books come out whole and dense — they need courageous readers to be traversed.

Influences: Kafka, Céline, Dostoevsky, Borges, Saramago, Pirandello, Cortázar, Perec, Remarque, Benni, Pessoa, Marías, Hamsun.

Cronache dal Sistema — Chronicles from the System2020
Dystopian satire · Novel · Italian

A man is erased from the System by a bureaucratic error. Without his identification number, he cannot work, call, pay, be imprisoned, or even be killed — the rifle won't fire without a number. The System that controls everything cannot process its own mistake. Dystopian satire in the tradition of Kafka and Orwell, but more desperate and funnier — because the protagonist is not a hero, he's a 350-pound man who just wants to watch the football match.

La trap rende stupidi? — Does Trap Music Make You Stupid?2026
Essay · Neuroscience & cultural criticism · Italian

Not a polemic — a neuroscience investigation. Peer-reviewed studies on neural entrainment, dopamine and musical pleasure, the measurable decline of harmonic complexity in popular music.
