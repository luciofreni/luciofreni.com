Il libro del Nulla

The Book of Nothing. Serious and semi-serious writings on the edge of existence

Lucio Freni · 2021 · fifty short pieces · Italian · English presentation

Because as long as things happen inside me, all is well: the ship runs straight, even through the storms… but a ship, unfortunately, is not only navigation, it is also and above all cargo… so, to unload and get a little life in a tavern or wherever, and to give this rickety sailing ship the very legitimacy of its need to exist, well… One has to come into port.

The book

Il libro del Nulla gathers fifty short prose pieces: some a page long, some a few lines, some complete short stories. It holds dialogue and aphorism, fable and confession together with a single posture, that of someone looking at existence from the edge, half in and half out, refusing to decide which side he belongs to.

It opens with Death paying a visit to a man dozing in the afternoon and arguing with him about permanent contracts and free will. It goes on to a world in which Time, for sport, removes one minute from every week, and people, rather than notice, throw away their watches. There is a bookseller who explains to a customer why the book she bought is no longer the same on second reading, a man who finds Maigret's pipe, an abandoned prison on an island that sends back reflections where there is no sun, a transparent man and a man made of pencil.

Between the stories sit the splinters: Riflessioni, Velocità, Mattino, Gli artisti, pages where the author speaks without a mask about his morning uselessness, about losing the thread of everything, about needing a constant supply of momentum so as not to fall prey to the gravitational fields of every passing asteroid. This is the register the author calls philosophical grotesque: comic and dark in the same paragraph, with metaphysics coming in through the service door of domestic life.

The declared references are Borges, Kafka, Cortázar and Perec, Hamsun, Céline; the tone is entirely Roman and entirely his own, an irony that does not console but keeps you company. The book is published in Italian only; the quotations below are English renderings, with page numbers from the 2021 Italian edition.

The author

Lucio Freni is an independent Italian writer based in Rome. His books span noir, dystopian satire, philosophical grotesque and epistolary fiction, and include Benzina, Cronache dal Sistema, Il libro del Nulla, Non torna mai nessuno da quaggiù and È tutta colpa di Dio.
