The book

Vorrei ("I would like...") is the poetry collection with which Lucio Freni made his debut, published by A & B in the Euterpe series (Acireale-Rome). It is the only one of his books to come from a third-party publisher; every later title is self-published. Because of that, it is also the only one of his works subject to Italy's legal-deposit requirement and catalogued in the national library system — see below.

The poems circle around desire — the wish named by the title, left open and unresolved — and the distance between what one would like and what happens. In the back-cover note, psychiatrist and essayist Alessandro Meluzzi describes the collection as "authentic philosophy", an expression of "love of knowledge" and "love of love". The book is published in Italian only; the passages above are English renderings of short factual material about the work.

A title held by Italy's National Library

Unlike Lucio Freni's self-published titles, Vorrei is catalogued in OPAC SBN — Italy's unified national library catalogue — under record ID IT\ICCU\PAL\0223998. A copy is held by the National Central Library of Florence, the National Central Library of Rome, and the Regional University Library of Catania. The author, "Freni, Lucio", also has his own authority record in the same catalogue (IT\ICCU\PALV\059671).

The author

Lucio Freni is an independent Italian writer based in Rome. After his poetic debut with Vorrei, he went on to publish short novels and collections spanning noir, dystopian satire, philosophical grotesque and epistolary fiction, including Benzina, Cronache dal Sistema and Il libro del Nulla. All titles are published in Italian. Author record on Wikidata.
